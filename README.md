# 🌍 EARTH'S SYMPHONY
### Don't Just See the Earth. Hear It.

**SONOVA | NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**  
**Challenge: The Earth Information Jukebox**

---

## ✨ Overview

**EARTH'S SYMPHONY** is an interactive web experience that transforms NASA's nighttime Earth observations into sound.

Instead of relying only on visual maps, users can explore Earth's night lights through musical notes. Brighter areas produce higher-pitched notes, while darker areas produce lower-pitched notes.

Our goal is to make Earth science more engaging and accessible, especially for blind and visually impaired users, students, and space enthusiasts.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🌐 Interactive Earth Explorer
Explore a 3D globe and hear how the intensity of nighttime lights varies between locations.

### ⏳ Time Machine
Listen to a sequence of **12 annual brightness values from 2012 to 2023**, represented as musical notes, and explore how a location's nighttime illumination changes over time.

### 🗺️ Visualize the Change
Explore NASA's nighttime-light change imagery to see areas where illumination has increased or decreased.

### 🎹 Sonify Your Map
Upload a nighttime-light image and transform its visual brightness patterns into sound, with piano- or guitar-style audio options.

## 💡 Why It Matters

Satellite maps contain valuable information about our changing planet, but they often depend heavily on visual interpretation.

**EARTH'S SYMPHONY introduces sound as another way to explore Earth data.**

Our project aims to support more inclusive learning experiences while making complex scientific information easier and more enjoyable to explore.

## 🔬 How It Works

1. We process NASA nighttime-light imagery covering **2012–2023**.
2. Image brightness is converted into relative values.
3. Brightness values are mapped to musical notes.
4. Users explore locations and years through an interactive visual and audio experience.

**Scientific note:** The brightness values are relative indicators derived from imagery. They are not calibrated radiance measurements, and changes in light intensity do not independently establish their underlying causes.

## ▶️ Run the Project

Clone or download this repository and open the project folder in **Visual Studio Code**.

Launch `index.html` using the **Live Server** extension, then select **Enter the Jukebox** to explore the experience.
